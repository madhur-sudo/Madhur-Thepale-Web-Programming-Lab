The Queue

by Basma Abdel Aziz

After a failed uprising, citizens must wait before an impenetrable Gate for permission to carry out even the simplest actions, including treatment for a man wounded by the authorities.

A darkly bureaucratic vision of power, paperwork, and the human cost of endless waiting.
