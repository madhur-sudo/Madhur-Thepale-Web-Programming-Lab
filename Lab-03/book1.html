The Memory Police

by Yōko Ogawa

Genre
Dystopian Fiction
Published
1994

On an unnamed island, ordinary objects disappear and most residents soon forget them. A novelist who still remembers hides her editor from the forces that enforce each new disappearance.

A quiet, unsettling story about memory, loss, and the fragile things that make up a life.
