The Housekeeper and the Professor

by Yōko Ogawa

Genre
Literary Fiction
Published

A housekeeper and her young son befriend a former mathematics professor whose memory lasts only eighty minutes, finding connection through numbers, baseball, and daily care.

A tender novel about the patterns that remain when memory cannot.
